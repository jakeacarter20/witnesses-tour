# Parley Street Travel Co. website

Static website for Parley Street Travel Co., LLC, selling seats on its first tour, **The Witnesses Tour**. It launches **November 2, 2026** at parleystreettravel.com.

## How the site works

- Plain HTML, CSS and a little vanilla JS, with no build step or framework, live on Vercel since 2026-10-30 at https://parleystreettravel.com (and www.), Vercel project `witnesses-tour` on team parleystreettravelco-4579, deploying automatically from the GitHub repo jakeacarter20/witnesses-tour on every push to main. GitHub Pages was the temporary preview and was turned off on 2026-10-31. Vercel Web Analytics (cookieless) is on since 2026-10-31: every page's head loads `/_vercel/insights/script.js`, so add those two script lines to any new page. Microsoft Clarity (project z6n9kx0wy9; heatmaps and session recordings, sets cookies) was added to every page's head on 2026-10-31, right after the Vercel lines; add it to new pages too. The chat panel carries data-clarity-mask so typed questions aren't recorded. privacy.html (live 2026-10-31, drafted for attorney review) discloses Clarity, Vercel Analytics, Stripe and what checkout collects, and every page's footer carries the Clarity notice with a Privacy policy link. Google Analytics (G-8CDX55VKBV) was added to every page's head on 2026-11-01, above the Vercel lines; add it to new pages too. thank-you.html strips ?session_id= from the address in an inline script that must stay above all analytics tags, because that ID opens the traveler details form. Update privacy.html if checkout fields, trackers or data sharing change. Your hosts has a carousel of 16 Instagram reels from @the.holy.rebellion (reels.js; edit the REELS list to change them). Each is Instagram's official embed, loaded only as it comes near the screen. Only one plays at a time: Instagram offers no pause control, so reels.js stops a video by reloading its embed (back to the cover) when another is clicked or when it leaves view; it knows which was clicked by watching document.activeElement, and on touch screens it stops any video that scrolls off. Welcome emails: a daily Vercel cron (vercel.json) calls /api/send-welcomes, which sends each paid deposit or full booking a welcome email through Resend two days after purchase (letter in api/_lib/welcome.js, settings in config.js; the waiver is sent with the traveler packet about a month before the trip, not in this email); it stays off until RESEND_API_KEY and CRON_SECRET are set in Vercel. Email sign-ups (signup.js on the tour page): a "Get trip updates" form in the footer and a "leave your email" form inside the Questions? chat (in its welcome message, and again when it can't answer or someone asks for a person). Instead of a pop-up, chat.js prompts once per visitor after 30 seconds or a third of the page (remembered in localStorage, skipped for anyone who already signed up): on desktop the chat opens without taking the keyboard focus; on phones a small "Have questions? Tap to ask" bubble appears above the chat button instead. /api/signup emails each one to hello@ (reply-to set to the visitor) and, when RESEND_CONTACTS_KEY (a full-access Resend key) is set, adds them to the Resend contact list for Broadcasts. Campaign emails need a physical mailing address (CAN-SPAM). A "Questions?" chat bubble (`chat.js`, loaded on index.html only, styles at the end of styles.css) answers from the page's own FAQ and sections, matched by keywords in its TOPICS list, with no AI and no server; anything it can't match offers an email to hello@parleystreettravel.com. If an FAQ question is reworded, update the matching `faq("...")` start text in chat.js.
- `api/` holds two Vercel functions (Node, Web-standard handlers, `stripe` 23.0.1): `stripe-webhook.js` verifies Stripe's signature and passes checkout events to `_lib/plan.js`, which schedules plan charges as draft invoices with `automatically_finalizes_at`, deletes them when superseded, and on `invoice.paid` moves a booking's remaining scheduled charges to a new card or bank account when a plan charge is paid with one (needs the webhook to send `invoice.paid` and the Stripe invoice setting "Save customer payment information" on); `seats.js` counts paid deposit and full seats for the banner. Dates live in `_lib/config.js`. `npm test` runs every `test/*.test.js` against fake Stripe clients. Secrets (STRIPE_SECRET_KEY as a restricted key, STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET, optional RESEND_API_KEY and ALERT_EMAIL) are set by the owner in Vercel only; never ask for them or put them in files.
- `index.html` is the single tour page. Its layout follows the Vita Travel retreat page (vita-travel.webflow.io/retreat) the owner chose as the model: breadcrumb and title over a faded engraving, then a content column beside a side box (price, dates, seats, section links, Book now) that stays in view on desktop and sits above the content on phones. It is our own code in the Parley Street colors and fonts; never copy that template's code, images or wording. `thank-you.html` is where Stripe redirects after payment. `terms.html` is a **draft outline** and must be replaced with attorney-approved terms before launch.
- Booking runs on **Stripe Payment Links**: 16 links, a deposit, pay-in-full, balance (rest after deposit) and payment-plan link for each of 4 room types. Stripe allows only 3 checkout questions, so the 8 booking links (deposit and full) ask the same 3 for every room: the buyer's date of birth, emergency contact name, emergency contact phone. They redirect to thank-you.html?session_id={CHECKOUT_SESSION_ID}, where traveler-form.js shows a form (one answer per box: each traveler's name, date of birth, email, phone; emergency contact; roommate choice for a solo Double) that /api/traveler-details saves onto the booking's Stripe Customer metadata (traveler_1_name and so on). The key needs Customers: Write for that. Balance and plan links still ask only for the name on the deposit booking.
- `styles.css` is linked as `styles.css?v=YYYYMMDD`; bump the date when the CSS changes so browsers don't serve a cached copy.
- `booking-config.js` is the only file to edit to open or close booking. It holds the deposit amount, the payment deadline, the fallback seats-sold count for the promo banner, the 16 link URLs and the sold-out flags. A room with an empty link shows "Booking opens soon".
- `SETUP.md` is the owner's step-by-step guide for Stripe, hosting and launch. Keep it in sync with any changes.

## Brand (from the brand guide; do not deviate)

- Colors: Olive `#4A5530` (main and backgrounds), Cream `#EFE9DA` (light backgrounds and text on olive), Dark Olive `#33391F` (text and line work on cream), Burnt Orange `#D98A52` (the sun, on olive only), Rust `#B4592B` (the sun and small accents, on cream). Olive and cream do most of the work; keep the oranges to the sun and small details.
- Type: Cormorant Garamond semibold for the name and headlines; Jost medium and regular for labels and body text. Small labels are set in capitals with wide letter spacing. Both fonts load from Google Fonts.
- Logo: the cream logo goes on olive and the dark logo on cream. Never stretch, recolor or redraw it. `assets/logo-horizontal-cream.svg` is file 08 (horizontal, reversed) and `assets/favicon.svg` is file 11 (icon on olive) from the owner's Final Logo Kit, with only the empty margin trimmed from the viewBox. `assets/apple-touch-icon.png` is rendered from the favicon.
- Images: the page header is a faded 1854 wood engraving, "Crossing the Mississippi" from Benjamin G. Ferris's Utah and the Mormons (public domain), recolored to Dark Olive with a transparent background (`assets/engraving-crossing-the-mississippi.png`); the owner chose it over a photo gallery. `assets/itinerary/` holds one 1000×1000 square photo per day, and `assets/meet-visitors-center.jpg` (EGDJ's public-domain 2010 photo from Wikimedia Commons, cropped to 3:2 and brightened) sits under Where we'll meet at up to 560px wide. Photos on churchofjesuschrist.org can't be used here: its terms of use bar commercial use without written permission. Photos come from Wikimedia Commons and Flickr under public domain, CC0 or CC BY-SA; the CC BY-SA ones legally require the credit line in the footer (`.photo-credits`), so keep it in step with any image you swap, and only use replacements whose license allows commercial use. Do not use Minerva Teichert or other 20th-century church art without written permission from the copyright holder. `assets/hero-art.jpg` is no longer on the page. The link-preview image is `assets/og-hero.jpg` (1200×630, a render of the hero: title over the engraving); `og:image` and `og:url` point at https://parleystreettravel.com/.

## Tour facts (keep the copy consistent with these)

- Dates: April 19 to 23, 2027. It begins at 1:30 PM on April 19 at the Independence Visitors' Center, 937 W Walnut St, Independence, MO 64050, and ends after the closing devotional at Carthage Jail at about 12:30 PM on April 23. Travelers choose their way home: the coach back to Kansas City (lunch on their own in Hannibal; MCI about 6:15 PM, Independence Visitors' Center about 7:00 PM), or leaving from Carthage on their own. Choices are collected after booking, by about March 1, 2027.
- Hosts: The Holy Rebellion (Christian Williams and Travis Lish, @the.holy.rebellion). Only use facts the owner has confirmed or that appear on their Instagram. Do not invent backstory.
- Capacity: 50 seats on one coach. The site cannot enforce this across Stripe links; the owner tracks it and sets the sold-out flags.
- Prices per person: Double $1,800 (base), Single $2,250, Triple $1,700, Quad $1,650. Nonrefundable deposit of $250 per person (changed from $150 on 2026-10-23; the $150 test links and prices were deactivated). By January 1, 2027, travelers must pay the rest in full or start a payment plan of 4 installments. Paying in full up front is also offered. Promotion: the first 25 seats sold get a free customizable Book of Mormon from The Holy Rebellion (the books: https://refugeproducts.com/scriptures). It shows in a cream banner that floats under the header while scrolling (its height is kept in the `--promo` CSS variable so the side box and section links stop below it); the banner tally comes from `/api/seats` (paid deposit and full checkouts in Stripe; refunds not subtracted), falling back to `seatsSold`. Checkouts listed in NOT_COUNTED in api/_lib/config.js are left out (Jaime's own Double deposit of 2026-10-31 is). Payment plan: the rest of the price in 4 equal payments, spaced evenly from the traveler's start date (right away, or any time up to January 1, 2027) to a final payment on March 19, 2027, one month before departure, auto-charged to a saved card or bank account. The deposit counts toward the price. A seat counts toward the 25 free books once its deposit is paid. Stripe: the test-mode build (sandbox account acct_1UTMRRPaF9TJbPkC, 4 products, 16 prices, 16 payment links: deposit, full, balance and plan per room) was created on 2026-10-23 and on 2026-10-30 they were copied to the live account acct_1UTTLNBGUzBPtYtu, whose 16 live links are in booking-config.js. Plan links charge payment 1 of 4 with setup_future_usage=off_session; the webhook schedules payments 2 to 4. Deposit links also save the payment method (setup_future_usage=off_session) and require a consent checkbox (consent_collection.terms_of_service with custom text): if the traveler hasn't paid in full or started the plan by January 1, 2027, the plan starts automatically, charged January 1, January 27, February 21 and March 19, 2027. The site has a matching acknowledgement checkbox that gates the deposit buttons. The webhook schedules auto-plan invoices per depositor and deletes them if they pay the balance or start the plan themselves (matched by email across customer records). The thank-you redirect goes to https://parleystreettravel.com/thank-you.html. Triples and quads book the whole room in one order. Solo travelers booking a Double are paired with a roommate.
- Included: motorcoach, 4 nights' hotel, 4 breakfasts, 3 group dinners (welcome dinner on Day 1, Day 2 in Chillicothe, a catered farewell dinner in Nauvoo on Day 4) and a picnic lunch, site visits with the hosts, snacks and drinks on the bus, dedicated time with the hosts, a chance to share your own witness, and a custom travel booklet (itinerary, stories, fun facts, journaling pages, bus activities; the site lists it only as "Custom travel booklet"). Not included: travel to and from the tour, other meals and travel insurance (the driver tip line was removed from the site on 2026-10-31). Travelers must be 16 or older.
- Theme: the Book of Mormon witnesses, the men (the tour visits several witnesses' graves; never promise a number of graves or name ones that aren't confirmed stops) and the women who witnessed it too (Mary Whitmer, Amanda Barnes Smith, Emma Smith, the Relief Society founders, and others).
