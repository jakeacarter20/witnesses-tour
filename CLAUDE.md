# Parley Street Travel Co. website

Static website for Parley Street Travel Co., LLC, selling seats on its first tour, **The Witnesses Tour**. It launches **November 2, 2026** at parleystreettravel.com.

## How the site works

- Plain HTML, CSS and a little vanilla JS, with no build step or framework, hosted on Vercel from the GitHub repo (jakeacarter20/witnesses-tour; the domain was bought through Vercel). GitHub Pages was the temporary preview and is to be turned off.
- `api/` holds two Vercel functions (Node, Web-standard handlers, `stripe` 23.0.1): `stripe-webhook.js` verifies Stripe's signature and passes checkout events to `_lib/plan.js`, which schedules plan charges as draft invoices with `automatically_finalizes_at` and deletes them when superseded; `seats.js` counts paid deposit and full seats for the banner. Dates live in `_lib/config.js`. `npm test` runs `test/plan.test.js` against a fake Stripe client. Secrets (STRIPE_SECRET_KEY as a restricted key, STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET, optional RESEND_API_KEY and ALERT_EMAIL) are set by the owner in Vercel only; never ask for them or put them in files.
- `index.html` is the single tour page. Its layout follows the Vita Travel retreat page (vita-travel.webflow.io/retreat) the owner chose as the model: breadcrumb and title over a faded engraving, then a content column beside a side box (price, dates, seats, section links, Book now) that stays in view on desktop and sits above the content on phones. It is our own code in the Parley Street colors and fonts; never copy that template's code, images or wording. `thank-you.html` is where Stripe redirects after payment. `terms.html` is a **draft outline** and must be replaced with attorney-approved terms before launch.
- Booking runs on **Stripe Payment Links**: 16 links, a deposit, pay-in-full, balance (rest after deposit) and payment-plan link for each of 4 room types. Traveler details are collected by custom fields in Stripe checkout, not by the site.
- `styles.css` is linked as `styles.css?v=YYYYMMDD`; bump the date when the CSS changes so browsers don't serve a cached copy.
- `booking-config.js` is the only file to edit to open or close booking. It holds the deposit amount, the payment deadline, the fallback seats-sold count for the promo banner, the 16 link URLs and the sold-out flags. A room with an empty link shows "Booking opens soon".
- `SETUP.md` is the owner's step-by-step guide for Stripe, hosting and launch. Keep it in sync with any changes.

## Brand (from the brand guide; do not deviate)

- Colors: Olive `#4A5530` (main and backgrounds), Cream `#EFE9DA` (light backgrounds and text on olive), Dark Olive `#33391F` (text and line work on cream), Burnt Orange `#D98A52` (the sun, on olive only), Rust `#B4592B` (the sun and small accents, on cream). Olive and cream do most of the work; keep the oranges to the sun and small details.
- Type: Cormorant Garamond semibold for the name and headlines; Jost medium and regular for labels and body text. Small labels are set in capitals with wide letter spacing. Both fonts load from Google Fonts.
- Logo: the cream logo goes on olive and the dark logo on cream. Never stretch, recolor or redraw it. `assets/logo-horizontal-cream.svg` is file 08 (horizontal, reversed) and `assets/favicon.svg` is file 11 (icon on olive) from the owner's Final Logo Kit, with only the empty margin trimmed from the viewBox. `assets/apple-touch-icon.png` is rendered from the favicon.
- Images: the page header is a faded 1854 wood engraving, "Crossing the Mississippi" from Benjamin G. Ferris's Utah and the Mormons (public domain), recolored to Dark Olive with a transparent background (`assets/engraving-crossing-the-mississippi.png`); the owner chose it over a photo gallery. `assets/itinerary/` holds one 1000×1000 square photo per day, and `assets/meet-visitors-center.jpg` (EGDJ's public-domain 2010 photo from Wikimedia Commons, cropped to 3:2 and brightened) sits under Where we'll meet at up to 560px wide. Photos on churchofjesuschrist.org can't be used here: its terms of use bar commercial use without written permission. Photos come from Wikimedia Commons and Flickr under public domain, CC0 or CC BY-SA; the CC BY-SA ones legally require the credit line in the footer (`.photo-credits`), so keep it in step with any image you swap, and only use replacements whose license allows commercial use. Do not use Minerva Teichert or other 20th-century church art without written permission from the copyright holder. `assets/hero-art.jpg` is no longer on the page. The link-preview image is `assets/og-hero.jpg` (1200×630, a render of the hero: title over the engraving); `og:image` and `og:url` point at the GitHub Pages address and must switch to https://parleystreettravel.com/ when the site moves to the domain.

## Tour facts (keep the copy consistent with these)

- Dates: April 19 to 23, 2027. It begins at 1:30 PM on April 19 at the Independence Visitors' Center, 937 W Walnut St, Independence, MO 64050, and ends after the closing devotional at Carthage Jail at about 12:30 PM on April 23. Travelers choose their way home: the coach back to Kansas City (lunch on their own in Hannibal; MCI about 6:15 PM, Independence Visitors' Center about 7:00 PM), or leaving from Carthage on their own. Choices are collected after booking, by about March 1, 2027.
- Hosts: The Holy Rebellion (Christian Williams and Travis Lish, @the.holy.rebellion). Only use facts the owner has confirmed or that appear on their Instagram. Do not invent backstory.
- Capacity: 50 seats on one coach. The site cannot enforce this across Stripe links; the owner tracks it and sets the sold-out flags.
- Prices per person: Double $1,800 (base), Single $2,250, Triple $1,700, Quad $1,650. Nonrefundable deposit of $250 per person (changed from $150 on 2026-10-23; the $150 test links and prices were deactivated). By January 1, 2027, travelers must pay the rest in full or start a payment plan of 4 installments. Paying in full up front is also offered. Promotion: the first 25 seats sold get a free customizable Book of Mormon from The Holy Rebellion (the books: https://refugeproducts.com/scriptures). It shows in a cream banner that floats under the header while scrolling (its height is kept in the `--promo` CSS variable so the side box and section links stop below it); the banner tally comes from `/api/seats` (paid deposit and full checkouts in Stripe; refunds not subtracted), falling back to `seatsSold`. Payment plan: the rest of the price in 4 equal payments, spaced evenly from the traveler's start date (right away, or any time up to January 1, 2027) to a final payment on March 19, 2027, one month before departure, auto-charged to a saved card or bank account. The deposit counts toward the price. A seat counts toward the 25 free books once its deposit is paid. Stripe: the test-mode build (sandbox account acct_1UTMRRPaF9TJbPkC, 4 products, 16 prices, 16 payment links: deposit, full, balance and plan per room) was created on 2026-10-23 and its test URLs are in booking-config.js; live-mode links still need creating before launch. Plan links charge payment 1 of 4 with setup_future_usage=off_session; the webhook schedules payments 2 to 4. Deposit links also save the payment method (setup_future_usage=off_session) and require a consent checkbox (consent_collection.terms_of_service with custom text): if the traveler hasn't paid in full or started the plan by January 1, 2027, the plan starts automatically, charged January 1, January 27, February 21 and March 19, 2027. The site has a matching acknowledgement checkbox that gates the deposit buttons. The webhook schedules auto-plan invoices per depositor and deletes them if they pay the balance or start the plan themselves (matched by email across customer records). The thank-you redirect uses the GitHub Pages URL until the domain is connected. Triples and quads book the whole room in one order. Solo travelers booking a Double are paired with a roommate.
- Included: motorcoach, 4 nights' hotel, 4 breakfasts, 3 group dinners (welcome dinner on Day 1, Day 2 in Chillicothe, a catered farewell dinner in Nauvoo on Day 4) and a picnic lunch, site visits with the hosts. Not included: travel to and from the tour, other meals, the driver tip and travel insurance. Travelers must be 16 or older.
- Theme: the Book of Mormon witnesses, the men (the tour visits several witnesses' graves; never promise a number of graves or name ones that aren't confirmed stops) and the women who witnessed it too (Mary Whitmer, Amanda Barnes Smith, Emma Smith, the Relief Society founders, and others).
