About the tour

Five days walking the ground of the Restoration, limited to 50 travelers on one coach and hosted by The Holy Rebellion.

We visit the classic sites, Liberty Jail, Far West, Adam-ondi-Ahman, Historic Nauvoo, the Nauvoo Temple, and Carthage Jail, and stand in the places where the Book of Mormon witnesses lived, testified, and are buried.

The tour runs April 19 to 23, 2027. Travelers must be 16 or older.

What's included in the tour

  • Deluxe motorcoach for all five days
  • 4 nights' hotel
  • 4 breakfasts
  • 3 group dinners
  • A picnic lunch at Adam-ondi-Ahman
  • Site visits and history with your hosts

The Witnesses

Stand at the graves of the men who testified of the Book of Mormon. Then, hear from the women who witnessed it too, and where it happened. They testified of a book written “to the convincing of the Jew and Gentile that Jesus is the Christ.” Every one of their testimonies points back to Him.

Full itinerary

Begins: Mon, April 19, 1:30 PM in Independence

Ends: Fri, April 23, about 12:30 PM in Carthage, or about 6:15 PM in Kansas City by coach

Times are approximate and will be confirmed in your traveler packet.

Day 1Mon, April 19

Independence

Gather at 1:30 PM at the Independence Visitors' Center. The Temple Lot, the Community of Christ Temple, the 1833 printing office site, and the courthouse square, then a welcome dinner with your hosts.

Day 2Tue, April 20

Liberty, Richmond & Far West

Liberty Jail, witnesses' graves and monuments around Liberty and Richmond, including those of David Whitmer and Oliver Cowdery, the Richmond courthouse square, and the Far West temple site. Lunch on your own on Liberty's historic square; group dinner in the evening.

Day 3Wed, April 21

Hawn's Mill, Adam-ondi-Ahman & on to Nauvoo

Hawn's Mill and Adam-ondi-Ahman with a picnic lunch, then east across Missouri to Washington Park in Quincy, Illinois. Dinner on your own in downtown Quincy, and an evening walk at the Nauvoo Temple.

Day 4Thu, April 22

Nauvoo

The Monument to Women garden, Historic Nauvoo, the Joseph Smith Homestead, Mansion House, Red Brick Store, and Smith Family Cemetery. A free afternoon for the temple, more sites, or Mulholland Street, then a farewell dinner and the Trail of Hope at dusk.

Day 5Fri, April 23

Carthage

A closing visit and devotional at Carthage Jail, where the tour concludes at about 12:30 PM. Ride the coach back to Kansas City, with a lunch stop in Hannibal, or leave from Carthage on your own.

Your hosts

Christian Williams & Travis Lish

The Holy Rebellion · @the.holy.rebellion

If you follow @the.holy.rebellion, you know what Christian Williams and Travis Lish do: confront the hard questions about The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, dig into what the record actually says, and answer as peacemakers. Their aim is to "dismantle the argument without dismantling the arguer," and to be a window, not a mirror.

Now they're taking it off the screen and onto the bus. You'll stand with them at Liberty Jail, the Whitmer graves, and Carthage, and talk through the history in the places it happened. Bring your questions, especially the hard ones.
