The tour

Five days walking the ground of the Restoration

Limited to 50 travelers on one coach, hosted by The Holy Rebellion.

The classic sites

Liberty Jail, Far West, Adam-ondi-Ahman, Historic Nauvoo, the Nauvoo Temple, and Carthage Jail.

The Witnesses

Stand at the graves of 10 of the men who testified of the Book of Mormon, including David Whitmer, whose headstone still carries his testimony. Then hear from the women who witnessed it too, where it happened: Mary Whitmer, the only woman known to have seen the gold plates, Amanda Barnes Smith at Hawn's Mill, Emma Smith, and the founders of the Relief Society in Nauvoo.

Itinerary

Day by day

Times are approximate and will be confirmed in your traveler packet.

Day 1
Mon, April 19

Independence

Gather at 1:30 PM at the Independence Visitors' Center. The Temple Lot, the Community of Christ Temple, the 1833 printing office site, and the courthouse square, then a welcome dinner with your hosts.

Her storyTeenage sisters Mary Elizabeth and Caroline Rollins rescued pages of the revelations as a mob wrecked the press, and hid on top of them in a cornfield.

Day 2
Tue, April 20

Liberty, Richmond & Far West

Liberty Jail, the Eight Witnesses monument, the graves of David Whitmer, Oliver Cowdery, Jacob and John Whitmer, the Richmond courthouse square, and the Far West temple site. Lunch on your own on Liberty's historic square; group dinner in the evening.

Her storyMary Fielding Smith and her sister Mercy Fielding Thompson travelled 40 miles in February so Hyrum could see his newborn son in Liberty Jail. In Richmond, Mary Whitmer, the only woman known to have seen the gold plates, rests beside her husband.

Day 3
Wed, April 21

Hawn's Mill, Adam-ondi-Ahman & on to Nauvoo

Hawn's Mill, Adam-ondi-Ahman with a picnic lunch, and Gallatin, then east across Missouri to Washington Park in Quincy, Illinois. Dinner on your own in downtown Quincy, and an evening walk at the Nauvoo Temple.

Her storyAmanda Barnes Smith at Hawn's Mill, Eliza R. Snow at Adam-ondi-Ahman, and Emma Smith crossing the frozen Mississippi to reach Quincy.

Day 4
Thu, April 22

Nauvoo

The Monument to Women garden, Historic Nauvoo, the Joseph Smith Homestead, Mansion House, Red Brick Store, and Smith Family Cemetery. A free afternoon for the temple, more sites, or Mulholland Street, then the Trail of Hope at dusk.

Her storyThe Relief Society began as a sewing society in Sarah Granger Kimball's parlor and was organized upstairs in the Red Brick Store in 1842.

Day 5
Fri, April 23

Carthage

A closing visit and devotional at Carthage Jail, where the tour concludes in the early afternoon.

Meet your hosts

The Holy Rebellion

If you follow @the.holy.rebellion, you know what Christian Williams and Travis Lish do: confront the hard questions about The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, dig into what the record actually says, and answer as peacemakers. Their aim is to "dismantle the argument without dismantling the arguer," and to be a window, not a mirror.

Now they're taking it off the screen and onto the bus. You'll stand with them at Liberty Jail, the Whitmer graves, and Carthage, and talk through the history in the places it happened. Bring your questions, especially the hard ones.

Before you go

Where we begin

Monday, April 19, 2027, at 1:30 PM at the Independence Visitors' Center, 937 W Walnut St, Independence, MO 64050.

Getting there

Fly into Kansas City International (MCI) by late morning on April 19. The visitors' center is about 40 minutes away by rideshare.

Where we end

Friday, April 23, in the early afternoon after Carthage Jail.

Walking

Expect moderate walking, including grassy cemeteries and uneven ground at rural sites. Carthage Jail has stairs and no elevator.

Meals

Breakfast each morning, two group dinners, and a picnic lunch are included. Other meals are on your own in towns with good local restaurants.

Travel insurance
