Parley Street Travel Co., LLC

Privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

This policy explains what information Parley Street Travel Co. ("we") collects through parleystreettravel.com and when you book The Witnesses Tour, how we use it, and who we share it with.

Information you give us

  • When you book: your name, email address, phone number, billing address and date of birth at checkout, then on our traveler details form each traveler's full name, date of birth, email and phone number, an emergency contact name and phone number, and your roommate choice.
  • Before the trip: what you share with us on your waiver and traveler forms, such as dietary or accessibility needs.
  • When you email us: your email address and whatever you write.
  • When you sign up on the site or ask us to reach out: your email address and any question you include.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card or bank details go directly to Stripe; we never receive or store your full card or account number. If you pay a deposit or start the payment plan, Stripe saves your payment method so later payments can be charged automatically, as described at checkout and in our terms.

How visitors use this site

We use three services to understand how people use the site so we can improve it:

  • Microsoft Clarity records how visitors move through our pages, including clicks, scrolling and mouse movement, and turns that into heatmaps and session replays. It uses cookies and similar technology to recognize returning visitors. Text typed into form fields and the site's chat is hidden from these recordings. Microsoft may use this data as described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • Google Analytics counts visits and shows which pages people view, how they found the site, and their approximate location, device and browser. It uses cookies to recognize returning visitors. Google may use this data as described in How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can opt out with Google's browser add-on.
  • Vercel Web Analytics counts page views and shows which pages are popular, which sites sent visitors, and visitors' country, device and browser. It doesn't use cookies.

The Your hosts section of the tour page shows videos from The Holy Rebellion's Instagram account. When those videos load, Instagram (Meta) may collect information about your visit and set cookies, as described in the Instagram Privacy Policy.

The "Questions?" chat on the tour page runs in your browser. What you type there isn't sent to us or stored.

How we use your information

  • To reserve your seat, room and hotel nights, and to run the tour
  • To take payments, including scheduled payment-plan charges
  • To send your receipt, traveler packet, waiver and trip updates
  • To reach your emergency contact if something happens on the trip
  • To answer questions you send through the site, and to send trip updates if you signed up. Every update includes a link to unsubscribe.
  • To improve the website
  • To keep business, tax and legal records

Who we share it with

  • Tour suppliers such as hotels, the motorcoach company, restaurants and historic sites, only what they need for the tour (for example, rooming lists and dietary needs).
  • Your hosts, The Holy Rebellion, who lead the tour and need traveler names to run it.
  • Service providers that run the site and our business, including Stripe, Vercel, Google, Microsoft, and Resend (which sends our emails and keeps our email list).
  • Others when required by law, or to protect travelers' safety.

We don't sell your personal information.

Your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing us. We may need to keep some records, such as payment records, for legal or tax reasons.

How long we keep it

We keep booking information for as long as we need it to run the tour and meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Children

Travelers must be 16 or older. This site isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
