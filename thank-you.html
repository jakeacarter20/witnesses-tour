What happens next

  1. Two days after you book, we'll email you a welcome letter.
  2. If you paid a deposit, the rest of your trip is charged automatically in 4 payments on January 1, January 27, February 21 and March 19, 2027. Want to pay it off sooner? Your welcome letter has a link to pay the rest any time before March 19.
  3. About a month before the trip, you'll get your traveler packet with final times, hotels, and roommate details, along with the traveler waiver for each traveler to sign.

Book your travel

Plan to arrive in Kansas City by late morning on Monday, April 19. We gather at 1:30 PM at the Independence Visitors' Center, 937 W Walnut St, Independence, MO 64050.

Get travel insurance within 14 days

We strongly recommend travel insurance. Compare plans from many insurers at Squaremouth. Buy it within 14 days of your first payment for the trip (your deposit, or your full payment): after that, most plans no longer cover pre-existing conditions or offer the option to cancel for any reason.
