Terms & cancellation policy

Booking and payment

A nonrefundable deposit of $250 per person reserves your seat. By January 1, 2027, the rest of the trip price must be paid in full, or a payment plan must be started. The plan splits the rest of the trip price into 4 equal payments, spaced evenly from the start date to March 19, 2027, and charged automatically to the traveler's saved card or bank account. Bookings made after January 1, 2027 must be paid in full. The deposit counts toward the trip price.

Automatic start of the payment plan

When you book with a deposit, your card or bank account is saved and you check a box at checkout agreeing to this: if you have not paid the rest of your trip in full or started the payment plan by January 1, 2027, the payment plan starts automatically, and the rest of the trip price is charged to that payment method in 4 equal payments on January 1, January 27, February 21 and March 19, 2027. You can avoid the automatic start by paying the rest in full or starting the plan yourself before January 1, 2027.
