Terms & cancellation policy

Booking and payment

A nonrefundable deposit of $250 per person reserves your seat. By January 1, 2027, the rest of the trip price must be paid in full, or a payment plan must be started. The plan splits the rest of the trip price into 4 equal payments, spaced evenly from the start date to March 19, 2027, and charged automatically to the traveler's saved card or bank account. Bookings made after January 1, 2027 must be paid in full. The deposit counts toward the trip price.

Rooms and roommates

Prices are per person. A Double, Triple or Quad booking covers every traveler in that room. If you travel solo and choose a shared room, we'll pair you with another solo traveler of the same gender. If we can't find you a roommate, you'll have the room to yourself at no extra cost.

Automatic start of the payment plan

When you book with a deposit, your card or bank account is saved and you check a box at checkout agreeing to this: if you have not paid the rest of your trip in full or started the payment plan by January 1, 2027, the payment plan starts automatically, and the rest of the trip price is charged to that payment method in 4 equal payments on January 1, January 27, February 21 and March 19, 2027. You can avoid the automatic start by paying the rest in full or starting the plan yourself before January 1, 2027.

Free Book of Mormon offer

Each traveler in a booking made before Thanksgiving (November 26, 2026), with a deposit or paid in full, receives a coupon code for a free customizable Book of Mormon from The Holy Rebellion, to use at custombookofmormon.com. That's one book per traveler, so a Triple receives 3 and a Quad receives 4. Travelers who later cancel can still receive their book.

Cancellations by travelers

  • The $250-per-person deposit is nonrefundable.
  • Cancel before March 19, 2027: you receive a full refund of what you've paid, minus the deposit.
  • Cancel from March 19 through April 12, 2027: you receive a refund of 50% of what you've paid beyond the deposit.
  • Cancel after April 12, 2027: no refund.

To cancel, email hello@parleystreettravel.com. The date we receive your email is your cancellation date.

Travel to and from the tour

Travelers are responsible for their own travel to the start of the tour and home from its end. We are not responsible for missed or delayed flights.

Travel insurance

We strongly recommend travel insurance.

Waiver and conduct

All travelers sign a waiver before departure.
